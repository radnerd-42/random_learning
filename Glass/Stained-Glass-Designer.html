User Guide & Instructions

🔍 View Controls (Zoom & Pan)

  • Cursor-Centered Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel up or down to zoom in or out. The view zooms directly toward your mouse pointer position.
  • Panning the Canvas: Press and hold the Middle Mouse Wheel (or hold Spacebar + Left Click) and drag to move across the workspace.
  • Reset View: Click "🎯 Reset Zoom & Pan" in the sidebar to return to default scale (100%).

✏️ Drawing Tools

  • Line 📏: Click once to set the start point, then click a second time to set the end point. Real-time length and angle readouts will display along the segment.
  • Free Draw ✏️: Click and hold the primary mouse button while moving across the screen to create continuous freehand paths. Release to complete.
  • 3P Arc 📐: Requires 3 clicks: 1st click sets Start Point, 2nd click sets End Point, and 3rd click defines the Arc Curvature Control Point.
  • Circle ⚪: Click once for the center point, drag out to desired size, and click again to confirm the radius.
  • S-Curve 〰️: Click start and end points to generate a smooth, fluid double-curve segment, ideal for organic glass borders.
  • Rectangle ▭: Click once for the top-left corner and click again for the opposite corner. Automatically breaks into individual editable line segments when using the Trim tool.

🛠️ Editing Tools & Selection

  • Select / Move ↖️: Click individual shapes or drag a selection box around multiple objects. Drag any highlighted object to move the entire selection while retaining proportions.
  • Trim ✂️: Click any segment bound by line/arc intersections to cleanly clip away only that specific section.
  • Eraser 🧹: Hover over any object (highlighted in red) and click to delete the full line or curve shape.
  • Mirror Horizontal ↔️: Flips selected object(s) horizontally across the center axis of their bounding box.
  • Mirror Vertical ↕️: Flips selected object(s) vertically across the center axis of their bounding box.
  • Copy & Paste 📋/📥: Duplicate selected elements across the canvas while keeping snapping and stroke alignments intact.

🧲 Snapping Features

  • Grid Snap: Magnetically snaps cursor to background grid intersections.
  • Endpoints: Snaps precisely to start/end points of existing lines, arcs, or freehand paths.
  • Midpoints: Snaps directly to center points of straight line segments.
  • Center Snap: Snaps to center points of drawn circles.
  • Snap Perpendicular: When drawing a line, snaps to form an exact 90° angle with target lines.

🖼️ Server Templates & Project Files

  • Server Templates (.gls): Select any pre-made pattern directly from the dropdown list. The application automatically fetches and loads the design onto your canvas as soon as a template is selected.
  • Save Project 💾: Downloads your current draft as a custom project file (.gls). Preserves line types, construction choices, paper dimensions, and precise shape coordinates.
  • Load Local File 📂: Upload any previously saved .gls project file from your computer to resume drafting.
  • Export PDF 📄: Generates a full 1:1 true-scale printable template with selected cut gap stroke widths.

📄 Paper & Project Setup

  • Settings Menu: Access Units Mode, Construction Type, and Paper Setup by clicking ⚙️ Settings in the top sidebar header.
  • Construction Type: Toggle between Lead Came (6px / 1/16" gap) and Copper Foil (4px / ~1/24" gap) to accurately calculate pattern gaps for your glass build.
  • Units Mode: Switch seamlessly between Inches (in) and Millimeters (mm).
  • Paper Setup: Choose standard paper sizes (US Letter, Legal, Tabloid, ISO A4, A3, A2) in Portrait or Landscape orientation.
  • Grid Size: Adjust the slider to dynamically change background grid spacing.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + C — Copy selected elements
  • Ctrl + V — Paste duplicated elements
  • Ctrl + Z — Undo last action
  • Ctrl + Y — Redo action
  • Delete / Backspace — Delete selected items
  • Esc — Clear selection or cancel active point buffers
  • Spacebar (Hold) + Drag — Pan across canvas
